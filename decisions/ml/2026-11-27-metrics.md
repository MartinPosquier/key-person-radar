Decision: out-of-sample R² is 1 - SSE(model) / SSE(constant benchmark), the benchmark being
a value fixed before the evaluated period (the training mean, or 0 for "no abnormal
return"); confidence intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over resampled
events (1,000 resamples, seed 42); the gain over B1 is tested by a paired bootstrap of the
per-event loss difference (absolute error by default), with a one-sided p-value = share
of resamples where the gain is <= 0. The sign hit rate is computed on non-neutral shocks.
F1 and recall use a 0.5 probability threshold by default.
Why: dossier §9.7 (metrics per target, bootstrap CIs with 1,000 resamples, "bootstrap of
the error difference" as the simple alternative to Diebold-Mariano).
Alternatives considered: sklearn r2_score (rejected: it benchmarks against the mean of the
test set itself, which is unknown ex ante and flatters the model); Diebold-Mariano test
(not needed: the events are not a single time series, and the paired bootstrap answers the
same question without a variance estimate of autocorrelated losses).
