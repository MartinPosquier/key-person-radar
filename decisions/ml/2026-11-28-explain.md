Decision: SHAP values are computed with TreeExplainer on the estimator of the best tree
pipeline of M7 (Random Forest or HistGradientBoosting, lowest validation MAE), on the matrix
that estimator actually sees (after the pipeline's preprocessing), for the validation
events. Importance = mean |SHAP| per feature, also summed per feature family. Lasso
coefficients are reported in CAR points (x100) per standard deviation. The error analysis
(MAE by sector, region, role, sign and year; the 20 largest absolute errors with an empty
`review_note` column to fill by hand) uses the validation split only; the same analysis on
the test split belongs to M12. Outputs go to processed/models/explain/ (CSV + PNG), not to
reports/, whose owner is not set in the playbook.
Why: dossier §9.8 (SHAP summary and dependence plots, Lasso coefficients, local explanation
of a famous shock) and §9.9 (errors by group, the 20 largest one by one, stability in time).
Alternatives considered: KernelExplainer on the whole pipeline (model-agnostic but slow and
approximate; TreeExplainer is exact for trees); SHAP on the training split (validation
shows how the model behaves on events it has not seen).
