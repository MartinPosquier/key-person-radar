Decision: scores pipeline (task M10), in three commands.
1. `scores sensitivity`: profiles = mean features of the person's events over the 3-year
   window [y-2, y]; standard shock z_wiki = 4 and tone at the person's own 10th / 90th
   percentile on that window, specificity and other variables at their mean (§3.1, §10.1).
   Method A = |best M7 model prediction| in % of CAR, interval from 200 bootstrap refits
   (5th-95th), then shrunk towards the role x sector peer group with k = 10 (§10.5; the
   interval moves with the point). Method B = |(beta + b_p) x delta tone| from the mixed
   model of the same window, normal 5th-95th interval from the slope's standard error
   (fixed + conditional random-effect variance); an interval crossing zero starts at 0.
   Agreement = mean of the Spearman correlations of A and B on S- and S+ each year (persons
   with both); above 0.6 publish the mean of A and B (raw values and bounds), else B; a
   person-year without B keeps A.
2. `scores value`: lambda is calibrated on the unwanted abrupt departures of
   config/natural_experiments.csv (wanted_by_company = false) dated up to the end of the
   training split (2021-12-31), each matched to the same company's event with the largest
   z_wiki within 10 days; departure shock = their mean z_wiki, tone and specificity; V
   interval = 500 draws combining the 200 model refits and resampled departures. Without
   any calibration departure, lambda = 1 and V uses the standard negative shock (logged).
   Pi uses market-adjusted returns (beta = 1) on J0..J0+2 of the person's events.
3. `scores smoothing`: 0-10 scale = piecewise-linear map between the 0th, 10th, ..., 100th
   percentiles of the 2015-2021 raw sensitivities, computed once into
   config/score_scale.yaml and frozen; smoothing w = 0.4 with a +/-2 yearly cap on the
   0-10 scale; in a mandate's first year (if after the first scored year) the previous value
   is the peer-group mean and no cap applies (succession_flag). Published bounds are the
   scaled raw bounds, widened if needed so they always contain the published score.
Default event splits: train + validation; the test split is added only after the M12 go.
Why: dossier §3.1-3.6 and §10.1-10.6.
Alternatives considered: bootstrap intervals for method B (200 MixedLM refits per window,
too slow for little gain over the analytic standard error); decile bins giving integer
scores (rejected: linear interpolation keeps the ranking within a decile).
