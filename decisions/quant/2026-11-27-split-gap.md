Session: quant
Decision: the 10-session gap of §9.5 at the fixed boundaries (train -> validation, validation -> test) is applied by flagging events whose J0 falls in the 10 business days before the boundary as excluded, reason `split_gap`; they keep their period label
Why: §9.5 ("un trou de 10 séances sépare l'entraînement de la validation, pour qu'un choc à cheval sur la frontière ne fuie pas"). The frozen schema allows only train / validation / test / reserved as split values, so a "gap" label would need a contract PR; the exclusion flag already removes them from every model fit and score, and keeps them countable. Business days (pd.offsets.BDay) match the ml session's ExpandingYearSplit, so the CV folds and the fixed split use the same gap definition.
Cost: about 20 business days out of ~2,750, under 1 % of events.
Alternatives considered: contract PR adding a "gap" split value (heavier, no extra benefit); exchange-calendar sessions instead of business days (a difference of at most one or two holidays, and it would diverge from the CV harness).
