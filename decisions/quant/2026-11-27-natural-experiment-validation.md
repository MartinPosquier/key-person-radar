Decision: ex ante validation (Q10) correlates the person's score at the end of the year before the event with |CAR[0, 2]| (Spearman, 95 % percentile bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 2026); main score v_pct, with s_minus and s_plus reported; naive benchmark = mean daily Wikipedia views of the person over the 365 days before the event; the model must beat it, judged by the paired bootstrap interval of rho(score) - rho(naive)
Why: §11.2 steps 2-5 ("corrélation de rang entre score ex ante et ampleur absolue du CAR réel, avec un intervalle bootstrap"; "comparer à un score naïf ... notre score doit faire mieux"). Magnitude, not direction, because a contested leader's departure can lift the price. v_pct first because it is the score of "how much of the company the person is"; the S scores are reported because their sign-specific meaning is useful for the arrest/death cases.
Details: if the person had no score at that company the year before (e.g. Niccol before Starbucks), the person's score at another company that year is used and marked `score_source = other_company`; a candidate without any prior score is kept in the table and left out of the correlation. person_id is matched from config/persons.yaml (id or alias) on the name slug (accents removed), which is the data session's convention.
