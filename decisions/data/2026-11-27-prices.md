Session: data
Decision: price tables from yfinance, as follows.
- `prices.ret` and `market.ret` are **log returns** of the adjusted close between consecutive
  trading days (dossier §6.4). The synthetic fixtures use simple returns; for daily moves the
  difference is negligible, but CAR code should treat `ret` as log returns. The quant session
  has been told through its inbox.
- `close_adj` = yfinance "Adj Close" (dividends and splits). Market indices use "Close".
- `market.index_id` is the yfinance ticker as written in `universe.yaml` (`^GSPC`, `^STOXX`).
- `shares_out` is split-adjusted (yfinance reports counts in the units of the day they were
  published) and forward-filled from the last report. Points more than 50 % away from the
  rolling median of their 15 neighbours are dropped as reporting errors. Before the first
  report (late 2015 for most companies, 2023 for STLAM) `shares_out` is missing. Block's
  counts before the 2025 ticker change come from the old ticker `SQ` (`former_tickers`).
- Prices stay in the listing currency (USD, EUR, GBp for UK lines, CHF, DKK). Market value
  = close × shares_out is therefore in local units; a USD conversion (needed for `v_usd`,
  dossier §3.5) is not in the frozen contract and would need an FX table (contract request
  if the ml session needs it).
- Extreme daily returns (|log ret| > ln 1.4) are kept: the 31 cases found (GME 2021, SNAP,
  NFLX 2022-04-20, ...) are real moves. Flagging and the -80 % / +200 % bounds are D10.
Why: dossier §5.4, §6.4, §6.7.
Alternatives considered: yfinance `auto_adjust=True` close for market value (rejected: it is
also dividend-adjusted, so close × shares would understate past market values).
