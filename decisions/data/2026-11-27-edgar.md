Decision: US earnings dates from SEC EDGAR (task D6)
- Source: EDGAR submissions API (data.sec.gov). Filers are matched through `sec_ciks` in
  universe.yaml: www.sec.gov refuses requests without a contact, so the ticker -> CIK file
  is not used; every CIK was checked by name. Google Inc. / Alphabet, TWDC / Walt Disney Co
  and the old / new BlackRock holding each have two CIKs.
- Date = `reportDate` of each 8-K with item 2.02, else the filing date. Every 2.02 filing is
  kept (results, preliminary results, guidance revisions, Tesla's quarterly delivery reports,
  Apple's 2019-01-02 revenue warning): all are company information releases, which is what
  the earnings filter of §7.4 must remove.
- Years with fewer than 3 such dates: 10-Q / 10-K filing dates are added when no 8-K 2.02 is
  within 10 days (Berkshire and Strategy publish results with the 10-Q): 39 dates.
- `quarter` = calendar quarter of (date - 75 days).
- Shopify, Spotify, BioNTech (6-K filers) go to the manual template (D9).
- Item 5.02 filings (1,006 officer changes) are saved in raw/edgar/officer_changes.csv for
  the natural experiments.
Result: 1,607 dates for 34 companies, about 4 per company-year (Tesla 8: deliveries).
