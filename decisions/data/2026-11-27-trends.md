Session: data
Decision: Google Trends ratios (task D7), best effort
- Terms: Google Trends entities (topic ids) when a suggestion has the exact name and a type
  that fits (person: CEO, executive, founder...; company: company, corporation, bank...),
  else plain text (dossier §4.5). 117 of 140 terms resolved to entities; the choice is in
  raw/google_trends/entities.json for review.
- Each person is compared with the company of their first mandate, weekly, in windows of at
  most 5 years: 2014-2018, 2019-2023, 2024-2025.
- ratio_person_company(year) = median over the weeks of the year of person / company,
  weeks where the company is at 0 skipped (dossier §4.4 step 2). The 0.10 threshold is
  applied in the panel selection (D11).
- Google answered HTTP 429 from the first series requests. The collector stops after 3
  consecutive 429 and resumes from its cache on the next run. Persons without a ratio are
  not filtered out on Trends (the views filter still applies).
Why: dossier §4.4, §4.5, §5.5 ("stop on repeated 429 errors").
Alternatives considered: plain text terms (rejected: homonyms such as "Tim Cook" the
historian or "David Solomon" the DJ).
